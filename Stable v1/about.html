About Stones of Time

Stones of Time is an open-world sandbox game, where the player must explore a vast world, collect resources, and grow powerful enough to defeat the Eclipse Disciple.

The current state of the game involves the official website being in active development, and the majority of the artwork and textures being complete. It is still in the early stages of development, and progress will be updated in the News section of this website.

Development Team

Diversity Studios is a small indie game development studio, founded by Ethan Boux. Although the studio is known for their small games such as Perplexity or their 2048 game recreation, they are best known for their passion game project, Stones of Time.

  1. Ethan Boux - Founder & Lead Developer
  2. Marcus Boux - Artist, Game Designer, and Storywriter

The studio hopes to acquire more members to expand their team, grow their community, and transform their projects into games that can be enjoyed by anyone.
